axon-tui

The keyboard-first terminal client for Axon, driven through a scripted tour of a seeded demo world.

What you are looking at

Everything on screen is fictional and reproducible: personas, rooms and messages come from a declarative corpus that a throwaway Synapse + Axon stack renders on demand, so the same recording can be re-shot after a UI change. The images really are drawn in the terminal — Sixel, Kitty or iTerm2 graphics, depending on what the terminal supports — not approximated with block characters.

The tour covers, in order:

  • the room list, with sort, filters and a live name filter
  • a timeline with formatted messages, replies and reactions
  • adding a reaction, then withdrawing it
  • threads, edits and redactions
  • inline photographs and a full-size preview
  • full-text search across rooms, narrowed by a room filter
  • jumping to a date in history
  • sending a message
  • the keyboard-shortcut and help popups

Reproduce it yourself with scripts/demo-stack.sh up then scripts/demo-stack.sh record — see smoke/tui/README.md and docs/demo-coverage.md, which tracks what each scene is meant to show.
